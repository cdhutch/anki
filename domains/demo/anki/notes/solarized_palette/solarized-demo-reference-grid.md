# back_md

<div class="swatch-role">
  Solarized's design: the 8 base tones swap roles between light and dark mode
  (base03&harr;base3, base02&harr;base2, base01&harr;base1, base00&harr;base0), while the
  8 accent colors (yellow through green) keep the <em>same</em> hex value in both modes by
  design &mdash; that's what lets code/text highlighting look consistent regardless of theme.
</div>
<div class="meta-row">Flip through the individual swatch cards in this deck (front: swatch + hex,
back: legibility preview on both a light and a dark chip) to judge each color on its own,
especially for low-light/mobile use. See the "How night mode detection works" card for the
AnkiMobile vs. macOS control-code findings.</div>
