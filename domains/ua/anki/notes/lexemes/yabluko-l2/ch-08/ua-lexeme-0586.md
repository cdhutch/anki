  ConfusableSet: гострий - both words translate English "sharp," but for different senses.
    гострий describes an intrinsic quality -- a physical edge (a sharp knife), spiciness (spicy
    food), or mental acuity (a sharp mind). різкий describes the manner of a change or action
    -- a sharp or abrupt turn, a sharp drop in temperature, a sharp pain of sudden onset,
    a sharp tone or smell. The two are not interchangeable -- гострий ніж (a sharp knife)
    cannot become різкий ніж, and різкий поворот (a sharp turn) cannot become гострий поворот.
    See ua-lexeme-0503.
  Mnemonic_EN: різкий = sharp as in HOW something changed (a jolt, a swerve, a sudden drop)
    -- not what a thing intrinsically is. Contrast гострий (ua-lexeme-0503), which describes
    WHAT a thing is (a blade, spicy food, a keen mind).
