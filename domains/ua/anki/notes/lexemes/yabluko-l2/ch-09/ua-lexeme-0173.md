  ConfusableSet: вого́нь (ua-lexeme-0329) and ва́тра (ua-lexeme-0610) -- convergent near-synonyms,
    not homographs. пожежа is specifically a destructive, uncontrolled fire -- a blaze or
    conflagration, the kind firefighters put out (see this note's own example). вого́нь is
    the broad, general/elemental word for fire itself (a flame, a campfire's light, bodily
    heat, gunfire) -- pure fire, not implying harm or intent. ва́тра is specifically a campfire/bonfire
    (Горох defines it as вогнище) -- a controlled, intentional fire built for warmth, light,
    or gathering. So пожежа is the "bad" fire, ва́тра is the "on-purpose" fire, and вого́нь
    is the umbrella term covering both plus everything else fire-related.
