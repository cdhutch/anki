  Topic: по-/про-/с- + ходити — Aktionsart Perfectives (Couplet, not Triplet)
  Text: по- attaches to indeterminate ходити to form a delimitative perfective, {{c1::походити}}
    ("to walk around for a bit," a brief/delimited stretch of the activity). про- attaches
    to ходити to form a durative perfective, {{c2::проходити}} ("to be on one's feet / walking
    for an entire stated stretch of time"). с- (voiceless allomorph of з-/зі- before х) attaches
    to ходити to form an itive perfective, {{c3::сходити}} ("to go somewhere and come back,
    a completed round trip"). None of these three meanings is compatible with the strictly
    one-directional determinate verb іти, so all three prefixes attach to the indeterminate
    stem instead, and none derives its own secondary imperfective — plain {{c4::ходити}} supplies
    the imperfective for all three, making each an aspectual couplet rather than the triplet
    pattern of ходити/іти/піти.
  Extra: 'Delimitative по-: Я походив парком. ("I walked around the park for a bit.") Same
    Aktionsart по- as почитати ("read for a while"), посидіти ("sit for a while") — a general
    "do X briefly" marker, unrelated to the inceptive по- that forms піти (see ua-grammar-0006).
    Homograph trap: походи́ти (perfective, stress on -ди́-, "walk around a bit") is a different
    word from похо́дити (imperfective, stress on -хо́-, "to originate, come from") — same
    spelling, different stress, different lexeme. The second word already exists in the corpus
    as ua-lexeme-3729, currently without a ConfusableSet flag — worth adding one. Durative
    про-: Він проходив цілий день. ("He was on his feet / walking the whole day.") Dual-life
    warning: проходити is ALSO the ordinary secondary imperfective of пройти ("to pass by/through"),
    built the regular way on the determinate -йти stem via про- — see ua-grammar-0002/ua-grammar-0003
    for the standard 9-prefix PVOM system. Same surface form, opposite aspect (one imperfective
    here, one perfective there), unrelated meaning — only context and the paired verb disambiguate,
    not spelling. Itive с-: Я сходив до магазину. ("I went to the store and came back.") Dual-life
    warning: сходити is ALSO the ordinary imperfective paired with perfective зійти ("to rise/ascend"
    — сонце сходить, "the sun rises"), unrelated meaning, again built the regular -йти way.
    Structural point: the regular PVOM prefixes (при-, в-, ви-, під-, від-, до-, про-, пере-,
    за-) always build a dedicated impf/pfv couplet on the determinate stem (e.g. приходити/прийти)
    — see ua-grammar-0002/ua-grammar-0003. По-, про-, and с- here work differently: they are
    Aktionsart modifiers of the INDETERMINATE stem, and because "do briefly" / "do for a whole
    stretch" / "go and come back" cannot describe an ongoing process, none of the three has
    a dedicated secondary imperfective — plain ходити covers the imperfective side of all
    three. Companion notes: ua-grammar-0006 (піти''s own polysemy, same chapter) and ua-grammar-0007
    (ходити''s three meanings, already lists these same three PFV partners without explaining
    the mechanism — this note supplies that). Conjugation tables: ua-verb-0001 (ходити), ua-verb-0002
    (іти), ua-verb-0004 (піти). Base motion-verb triplet lexeme note: ua-lexeme-0581.'
